## What went well?

## What did not go well?
- Missing-deadline acceptance conditions were ambiguous when the simulation started[cite: 123].
- Demo preparation and review capacity were estimated too optimistically[cite: 123].
- Work was initially estimated before checking architectural dependencies[cite: 123].

## Root cause
Backlog refinement was too brief: each selected PBI lacked a confirmed example, owner for follow-up questions, and one testable acceptance check[cite: 123].

## One specific process improvement
**Before selecting a PBI for the next Sprint, spend 10 minutes on a refinement checklist:**
1. Identify the user value and one example input/output[cite: 123].
2. Write at least one testable acceptance condition[cite: 123].
3. Identify one dependency/risk and assign a contact person[cite: 123].
4. Confirm that the estimate still makes sense[cite: 123].

- **Owner:** Scrum Master facilitates; Product Owner and Developers participate[cite: 123].
- **When:** before the next Sprint Planning[cite: 123].
- **Success measure:** 100% of selected PBIs have at least one agreed acceptance example and documented dependency before task breakdown[cite: 123].
